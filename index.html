Frequently Asked Questions & Product Use Guidance

Realistic hair care expectations and regimen best practices.

How long does hair care product consistency take to show noticeable changes?

Hair growth cycles naturally occur over months. Most topical hair care regimens require 90 to 180 days of continuous uninterrupted daily use before visual hair density changes can be evaluated.

What should I do if I miss a daily application?

If you miss a dose, simply continue your regular schedule the following day. Do not apply double quantities to compensate for a missed application.

How should I apply scalp tonics or serums?

Apply directly onto a clean, dry scalp focusing on targeted thinning areas. Gently massage with your fingertips for 60 seconds to ensure even distribution.
