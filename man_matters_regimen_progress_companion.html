Frequently Asked Questions & Guidance

When can I expect visible hair density improvements?

Hair growth cycles take time. During Month 1, active ingredients work beneath the scalp to reactivate dormant hair follicles. Minor shedding may occur as weak hairs make room for strong new roots. By Month 3, scalp coverage increases visibly.

What happens if I forget to apply my tonic for a day or two?

Don't stress! Simply resume your normal dose the next morning. Do not double your dose. Using your Regimen+ app reminders will help keep your habit streak consistent over 90 days.

How should I apply the Man Matters Hair Tonic for best absorption?

Ensure your scalp is clean and dry. Spray 1ml directly onto thinning areas. Gently massage with your fingertips for 60 seconds to enhance blood circulation. Leave it on for at least 3 to 4 hours.
